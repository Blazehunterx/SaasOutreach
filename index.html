What is FounderFlow?

FounderFlow is a self-serve CRM and conversation platform for Instagram-first businesses: verified prospect research lists delivered to your dashboard, an AI assistant that drafts every reply in your voice for your review, follow-up cadence tracking across Instagram, LinkedIn, and Email, and pipeline management with booking sync.

Product description: Customers receive a web-based CRM dashboard plus optional research services. Features include: (1) Prospect Research — niche-targeted, verified contact lists prepared for your business and imported into your dashboard; (2) Growth Program — a written engagement and content cadence plan implemented with you; (3) Conversation Inbox — manage and qualify inbound DMs in one place with AI-drafted reply suggestions; (4) Follow-Up Cadence — structured follow-up reminders and tracking so no conversation is lost; (5) Complete Platform — everything combined. All plans include a CRM pipeline, an AI assistant that drafts replies in your brand voice for your review, Calendly/Cal.com booking sync, and customer support at marvin.2000.sluis@gmail.com. You remain in control of your Instagram account at all times: messages are sent from your own account, and you are responsible for complying with Instagram's Terms of Use.

Common Questions

How is my Instagram account protected?

FounderFlow is built to keep your account safe: human-paced sending, per-account limits, warmup ramp for new accounts, and instant pause if anything is flagged. You remain in control of your own account at all times, and you are responsible for complying with Instagram's Terms of Use.

Does the AI Assistant really sound like me?

The AI Assistant is trained on your persona, your tone, your product, and your angle. It drafts every reply fresh — no templates — asks questions to keep conversations moving, and suggests your calendar link when someone shows genuine interest. You review before anything is sent.

What niches does this work for?

FounderFlow works for any personal brand on Instagram, coaches, fitness professionals, consultants, real estate agents, creators, therapists, musicians, and small business owners. If you sell something and your clients are on Instagram, it works.

How much time does this save?

Most founders spend 2-3 hours a day on manual outreach. FounderFlow reduces that to about 15 minutes, just checking your pipeline and showing up to booked calls.

Do I need technical skills to use this?

No. You download the connector, log into your Instagram through it, and it securely syncs your session to your workspace. You just monitor results on the dashboard. Everything is shown in plain English: prospects added, conversations tracked, replies drafted, calls booked.

What services does FounderFlow offer?

We offer five services: Prospect Research for building contact lists, Growth Program for audience growth, Conversation Inbox for managing and qualifying inbound DMs, Follow-Up Cadence for never losing a conversation, and the Complete Platform with everything included.
