Our Story

We built FounderFlow because manual client acquisition doesn't scale.

It's tedious, it's slow, and it takes time away from the work that actually matters.

The Problem

Before building FounderFlow, I worked as a setter — sending DMs manually for hours every day. Copy-pasting the same messages, tracking who replied in a spreadsheet, following up with leads who went quiet. It worked, but it was soul-crushing.

I was spending 3+ hours a day on outreach alone. That's 15+ hours a week just sending messages — time I could have spent on strategy, product, or actually closing deals.

The tools that existed were either too expensive, too complicated, or built for agencies (not individual founders). I wanted something simple: a system that finds leads, sends DMs, handles replies, and books calls — without me touching anything.

The Solution

So I built it. FounderFlow is now a CRM and conversation platform for Instagram-first businesses with five services:

1. Prospect Research — Targeted, verified prospect lists researched for your niche and imported into your dashboard.

2. Growth Program — A growth cadence with content, engagement targets and reporting, implemented together.

3. Conversation Inbox — A single workspace to manage and qualify inbound DMs, with AI-drafted reply suggestions.

4. Follow-Up Cadence — Structured follow-up reminders and tracking so no conversation is lost.

5. Complete Platform — Everything combined: Prospect Research + Growth Program + Conversation Inbox + Follow-Up Cadence.

The result? Instead of 3 hours a day juggling DMs, you spend 15 minutes checking your pipeline and showing up to booked calls.

Who It's For

FounderFlow is built for personal brand builders — coaches, creators, fitness professionals, consultants, real estate agents, and small business owners who want to grow their Instagram and their business at the same time.

If you're currently tracking conversations in spreadsheets and juggling DMs by hand, and you want that time back — this is for you. Whether you need prospect research, growth planning, or a full conversation pipeline, we have a service that fits.
