Our Story

We built FounderFlow because Instagram growth shouldn't be manual.

It's tedious, it's slow, and it takes time away from the work that actually matters.

The Problem

Before building FounderFlow, I worked as a setter — sending DMs manually for hours every day. Copy-pasting the same messages, tracking who replied in a spreadsheet, following up with leads who went quiet. It worked, but it was soul-crushing.

I was spending 3+ hours a day on outreach alone. That's 15+ hours a week just sending messages — time I could have spent on strategy, product, or actually closing deals.

The tools that existed were either too expensive, too complicated, or built for agencies (not individual founders). I wanted something simple: a system that grows your Instagram audience, handles inbound leads, sends DMs, and books calls — without me touching anything.

The Solution

So I built it. FounderFlow is now a full Instagram growth engine with five services:

1. Lead Scraper — Extracts, filters, and exports targeted leads from your niche automatically.

2. Organic Growth — Automated follow/unfollow engine that grows your audience passively while you sleep.

3. Inbound Handler — AI-powered DM auto-reply that qualifies leads and books calls from your existing traffic.

4. Outbound Engine — Complete cold outreach pipeline: scraping + personalized DMs + AI replies + call booking.

5. All-in-One Growth Suite — Everything combined: Lead Scraper + Organic Growth + Inbound Handler + Outbound Engine.

The result? Instead of 3 hours a day on DMs, you spend 15 minutes checking your pipeline and showing up to booked calls. Your audience grows while you focus on what matters.

Who It's For

FounderFlow is built for personal brand builders — coaches, creators, fitness professionals, consultants, real estate agents, and small business owners who want to grow their Instagram and their business at the same time.

If you're currently doing your own DMs (or paying someone to do it), and you want that time back — this is for you. Whether you need organic growth, inbound handling, or full outbound outreach, we have a service that fits.
