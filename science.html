Under the Hood

How the platform works

A technical look at the systems that power FounderFlow — from prospect research to pipeline management.

01. Prospect Research

Prospect Research builds targeted, verified contact lists for your niche — delivered to your dashboard ready to contact. We research accounts that match your target profile, confirm contact details, and import qualified contacts into your workspace.

You control the niche, the filters, and the volume. Lists refresh on a monthly cadence so your pipeline always has fresh prospects.

Niche targeting Verified contacts CSV import Monthly refresh

02. Growth Program

The Growth Program builds your audience with a real cadence: content plan, engagement targets, and audience tracking - implemented together and reviewed together. Built on real engagement with your audience, not growth hacks.

Runs during idle time, never interleaved with conversation work. Conservative limits and human-paced engagement keep your account safe while growing your follower count.

Content cadence Engagement targets Audience tracking Monthly review

03. Conversation Inbox

When someone DMs you, the Conversation Inbox brings it into one workspace. It qualifies prospects, surfaces full history, and prepares AI-drafted reply suggestions — you decide what to send. Perfect for accounts with existing organic traffic.

Powered by the same AI Assistant, it reads incoming messages, drafts natural replies in your voice, and suggests your Calendly link when intent is detected.

Single inbox Lead qualification AI-drafted replies Calendly booking

04. Follow-Up Cadence

The Follow-Up Cadence keeps conversations moving with structured reminders and tracking. Each prospect gets a 3-step cadence with gentle nudges on a schedule you control, all logged in your workspace.

You decide who to message, when, and what to send — the system keeps it organized, tracked, and on schedule. No spam, no harassment.

3-step cadence Scheduled reminders Conversation tracking Safety-first

05. AI Assistant

When a prospect replies, the AI Assistant prepares the next reply. Powered by Gemini, it reads the incoming message, drafts a natural response in your voice, and suggests your calendar link when someone shows genuine interest. You review before anything is sent.

The AI is trained on your persona, your product, and common objections. It knows when to share more info, when to ask qualifying questions, and when to suggest booking. It also detects intent — mapping each conversation to a pipeline status (replied, meeting booked, not interested, etc.).

Gemini-powered Persona-trained Intent detection Calendly integration Objection handling

06. CRM Pipeline

Every contact is tracked through a clear pipeline: Discovered → Contacted → Replied → Meeting Booked → Qualified → Closed. You can see exactly where every conversation stands, filter by status, and update stages yourself or let the AI suggest updates.

The dashboard shows real-time stats: contacts added, conversations tracked, reply rates, calls booked. You can also see the full conversation history for any contact — every message sent and received, all in one place.

Real-time pipeline DM history Status tracking Analytics

07. Account Safety

FounderFlow is built to keep your account safe: human-paced sending limits, warmup ramp for new accounts, and instant pause if anything is flagged. You remain in control of your own account at all times.

Warmup ramp starts at 30% of your daily volume and increases over 14 days, so new accounts are protected from day one.

Real browser session Warmup mode Action block detection Human-like typing Crash protection
